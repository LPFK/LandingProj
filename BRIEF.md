# BRIEF

Short human brief. Edit freely. The authoritative spec is CLAUDE.md.

## What this is

A lead-capture landing page for a subscription service selling children's shoes
in France. The service is not live. The page's single job is to build a
qualified waitlist of parents who want early access.

## Who it is for

French parents of children age 0 to 7, urban to peri-urban, digitally
comfortable, sensitive to child foot health and to household logistics.

## The promise

The right shoes, the right size, delivered at the right cadence, without you
thinking about it.

## Tone

Modern, warm, editorial. Not corporate, not childish. Short sentences, present
tense, concrete nouns, honest. French for all visible copy.

## Success

Primary: qualified email signups. Secondary: time-on-page and scroll depth.

## Not in scope

No checkout, no product catalog, no login, no dashboard.
